# Model card: claim-triage-tabular v1

Generated by `make model-card` from the served champion; every number is measured on the untouched test split. Do not edit by hand: re-run it after a promotion.

## Intended use

- **Task:** predict whether a new motor claim will cost more than €5,000 and route it to a senior adjuster when the probability is at least the decision threshold (0.083431).
- **Users:** claims handlers triaging new claims. Each routed claim comes with reason codes (below).
- **Not for:** pricing, accepting or denying a claim, or judging the person who claims. Routing only decides who looks at the claim first.

## Explainability

Reason codes come from TreeSHAP, built into LightGBM: each claim's score splits exactly into one contribution per feature. The served model is ONNX, so it is rebuilt as LightGBM from its training recipe, and every explanation first checks that the rebuild predicts the same (largest difference here: 1.4e-07). The nightly batch job stores up to 3 reasons per claim in its `reasons` table.

## Limitations

- `exposure` is the top reason for 232 of 301 routed claims (77.1%): routing leans on one feature.
- driver_age under 25: routed at 15.4% vs 7.6% overall, precision 2.2%, calibration 1.512 (the model overstates this group's risk).
- region R93: routed at 23.4% vs 7.6% overall, precision 13.9%, calibration 0.901 (the model understates this group's risk).
- The fairness check has little power: 184 high-cost test claims, and recall intervals as wide as 5.1% to 47.7%. No alert is not proof of equal treatment.
- Reason codes explain the model, not the world: a feature raising the score is not evidence that it causes costly claims.
- The rebuild depends on the training data version (DVC). If the data changes, the parity check fails and no reasons are produced, by design.
